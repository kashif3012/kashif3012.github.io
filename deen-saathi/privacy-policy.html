Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Deen Saathi handles local data, device permissions, and third-party services.

App: Deen Saathi Last updated: May 16, 2026 Contact: imaanix.app@gmail.com

Overview

Deen Saathi is built to work with minimal data. We do not require an account, and we do not run our own analytics or profile system.

Most app preferences are saved locally on your device so the app can remember your choices between launches.

Information We Store on Your Device

The app may store settings such as language, theme, prayer preferences, madhab, manual location, planner preferences, notification settings, favorites, and Tasbeeh state on your device.

This information stays on the device unless your browser, device, or operating system syncs it as part of its own features.

Location and Prayer Times

If you allow location access or enter a city manually, Deen Saathi uses that location to calculate prayer times and show related prayer information.

  • Prayer time calculations use the Aladhan API.
  • City search and reverse geocoding may use OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
  • The Qibla view may use your device location to help point toward the Kaaba.

Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the app may ask for a few device permissions.

  • Location permission for prayer time and location-based features.
  • Notification permission for prayer reminders.
  • Exact alarm access on Android for more timely prayer notifications.
  • Motion or sensor access only where needed for compass-style views.

Third-Party Services

Deen Saathi uses a few third-party services to provide app features. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob for ads when ads are enabled.
  • Aladhan for prayer time data.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim for location search and city lookup.
  • Google Fonts for web-based typography.
  • Play Store, GitHub, and similar external links when you tap update, review, or promotional actions.

Ads and Promotions

When ads are enabled, AdMob may collect and process device or advertising identifiers in line with Google’s own policies. You can also disable ads from the app’s configuration when supported.

The app may show optional promotions for related products, but those links only open when you tap them.

Children

Deen Saathi is designed as a general audience Islamic utility app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own backend, because the app does not use one.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes, third-party services change, or new features are added. The latest version will be shown in the app.
